Privacy

Effective September 17, 2026. Applies to playletra.com and, when it is available, the Letra app.

The short version

Letra does not collect personal information from anyone, child or adult. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no advertising SDKs. Your kid's progress lives on your device and nowhere else.

What Letra collects

Nothing. We do not ask for a name, an email, a location, contacts, photos, or anything else about you or your child. There is nothing to log into.

What stays on your device

Trophies, progress, the chosen avatar and the volume settings are saved in your browser's local storage (or, in the app, on the device). They never leave it. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, wipes them.

What we count

The website keeps a handful of anonymous totals so we can tell whether the game is working for people: how many times a round was started, a word was finished, and whether the device that did it had played before. Each count is a plain tally with nothing attached to it. We do not record an IP address, a device identifier, a cookie, or anything that could connect a count to a person or a device.

playletra.com also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint devices, and does not follow anyone across sites.

The Letra app contains neither of these. It counts nothing.

The email list

If you type your email into the form on playletra.com, it goes to Buttondown, the newsletter service we use, and we use it for one thing: telling you Letra news, such as when the app is out. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing deletes your address. We never sell or share it.

Fonts

The website loads its two fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, that means Google's servers see the IP address of the device asking for them. The Letra app ships with its fonts inside it and makes no such request.

Ads

None, ever. Letra has no advertising and no advertising SDKs, and never will.

The Letra app

When the app is available, its one purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or your Apple ID. To restore an unlock on a new device, a purchase-processing service keeps an anonymous identifier for the app installation and the fact that the purchase was made. It is not linked to you and it is not used for advertising.

Children

Letra is made for children aged three to six, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, because we do not collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us information somehow, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing is kept. To erase progress, clear your browser data or delete the app. To leave the email list, click unsubscribe in any email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: hello@playletra.com.
